D17 refused a method on any recorded control arm outside [0.50, 2.00] on trades or turnover, and a
recorded arm is recorded forever -- trials carries append-only triggers. So the remedy D17's own
refusal message names ("re-draw it and amend") could never clear the refusal it caused: nine
dev-eligible methods would have stayed blocked by an arm that had already been replaced.

The rule now has a supersession clause. A REFUSE or WARN finding is superseded -- reported, never
dropped -- when the same method holds another placebo arm that is itself clean on both axes AND
that NAMES it in its own pre-registered declaration (Candidate.supersedes, read duck-typed through
control_shape.SUPERSEDES_ATTR). refusals() excludes it; `lab control` still prints it with its
ratios, names the arm that superseded it, and prints that arm's own resolved target beside it
where the two differ.

DECLARED, and deliberately not inferred from the target. "Another clean placebo on the same
target_id" was the obvious rule and it fails in both directions on these very nine. Too strict:
the <target-id>-PLC naming re-points a re-drawn arm at the book it actually controls, so
M0047-MV10-PLC resolves to M0047-MV10 while the arm it replaces resolves, on the ladder's weakest
rung, to M0047-EQ -- target equality would have left M0047 and M0202 blocked after every
measurement was taken. Too loose: M0215-HASH and M0215-HASH-CAP4 both resolve to M0215-CTRL, so
one honest control of CTRL would have excused an arm whose job is to price what a cap does -- and
the measurement shows exactly why that would have been wrong, since CAP4-PLC earns 0.268 points a
year MORE than CTRL-PLC. A resolution the module itself labels a guess is not a fact to build a
gate rule on; a declaration written before any result exists is.

Measured outcome, 2026-10-20, read-only: ten of the fourteen old arms are superseded and four are
not. The four that are not give four distinct causes, and only one of them is a mistake of any
kind:
  - M0221: structural. Turnover on a cap-weighted book is a property of the weight VECTOR, not the
    names. M0221-E50 pays 1.60x turnover holding C50's exact names and recording its exact 360
    trades; the re-drawn control pays 1.95x on 1.03x name changes.
  - M0201: a near miss. 0.79410 turnover against a 0.80000 bar, 0.0059 short, on one of two seeds
    -- the other seed landed at 0.839 and did supersede.
  - M0208: the entry-count estimator. The only miss on the TRADES axis in the whole set: one
    unrolled step of a keep band off a freshly-derived top-N under-counts the churn of a band
    whose held set has drifted, measured at 0.487x over 169 decisions and uniform across eras.
  - M0225: never drawn. Its twin's draw had already missed on the identical chassis, so invariant
    13 stopped the phase rather than spending a run to measure the same structural miss twice.

What this deliberately does NOT change: the bands do not move (WARN_BAND stays 0.80-1.25,
REFUSE_BAND stays 0.50-2.00); a `warn` SUPERSEDER does not excuse anything, which is exactly what
keeps M0201, M0208 and M0221 refused; an arm that declares nothing excuses nothing, which is every
arm the lab held before this batch; an unresolved or unrecorded arm is still never a refusal; and
there is still no override. A test asserts the clause is a no-op on the lab as it stood before any
honest control existed -- all nine still failed D17 then, and no arm declared anything at all.

And the line that keeps this honest: unblocking D17 promoted nothing and made nothing promotable.
hardgate.check tests the walk-forward folds BEFORE D17, so for M0201, M0221 and M0225 the live
D17 refusal is inert -- it is never the sentence lab promote prints. Of the nine, only M0208 has
D17 as its operative clause, and behind D17 stands the kin rule (M0002 read test-failed). Every
one of the nine is still refused today, eight of them on the fold majority. What the clause buys
is that the lab can never again be in a state where a recorded, append-only arm blocks a book that
has already replaced it -- and it buys it for the price of no method ids at all.
