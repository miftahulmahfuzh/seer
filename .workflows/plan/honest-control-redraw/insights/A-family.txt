Six of the thirteen D17-blocked control arms traded far too LITTLE -- 0.07x to 0.55x the book's
own trade count (M0227-R20 at 0.07x, M0225-R50 0.19x, M0221-R50 0.21x, M0215-HASH and
M0215-HASH-CAP4 0.34x, M0047-PLC1-MV10 and -MV20 0.55x). The mechanism was one error repeated:
the draw's hash carried no date term at all, or was re-keyed on a calendar quarter, so the
control's book changed only when the eligible pool did. A control that trades a fifteenth as
often pays a fifteenth of the fee bill, so it is not the book's shape with the identity removed
-- it is a cheaper strategy, and every margin quoted against it was a margin against a handicap.

Pre-registered direction, written into each Amendment before any result was seen: an honestly
shaped control pays the real fee bill, so it earns LESS, so the book's lead WIDENS or its deficit
NARROWS. Measured in trial_funding.mwr against SPY TR 7.188 %/yr on the same deposits:

  old control        h     old margin   new margin   moved as predicted?
  M0227-R20        0.07x      +5.220       +3.903    NO  (the control earned MORE: 7.733 -> 9.050)
  M0221-R50        0.21x      -1.090       -0.420    yes (deficit narrowed, as predicted)
  M0215-HASH       0.34x      +1.962       +2.163    yes (but by 0.20, not the predicted 0.3-1.5)
  M0215-HASH-CAP4  0.34x      +1.727       +1.613    NO  (reversed: the honest control earned more)
  M0047-PLC1-MV10  0.55x      +1.283       +3.333    yes (overshot its predicted band by 0.33)
  M0047-PLC1-MV20  0.55x      +0.533       +2.427    yes

Score: 4 of 6 arms, and 3 of 5 methods, moved in the pre-registered direction. The two misses are
at the OPPOSITE ends of the family, which is what makes them informative rather than noise.
M0227-R20's 41 trades in nineteen years were not a cheap book, they were a STALE one: twenty names
drawn in 1996 and essentially never revisited, whose concentration drag outweighed the fee bill it
avoided. Paying fifteen times the fees to redraw the same blind book was worth +1.32 points a
year. So on that chassis the sticky draw's handicap ran the opposite way from the one the whole
family assumed.

M0221 and M0225 were the hardest case, because there the handicapped control already BEAT its
book (-1.09 and -0.89 pts): the family-A prediction is that the deficit narrows, and that is the
one that could have turned a losing book into a winning one. On M0221 it narrowed to -0.42 and
did NOT cross zero. M0225 was never re-drawn at all -- its chassis is cap-weighted and the twin's
draw had already missed turnover at 1.95x -- so its -0.89 is still quoted against an arm trading
0.19x as often and remains the one unrepaired sentence in the set.

One consequence to state rather than bury: M0047-EQ now has NO control arm at all. Its only two
placebos were size-weighted arms the ladder mis-aimed at it by first-declared, and both were
replaced by arms aimed at MV10 and MV20 where they belong. So the standing sentence about EQ's
+3.80-point lead over its placebo is WITHDRAWN rather than re-measured: it was never supported by
a correctly-aimed control. A method arm with no placebo produces an empty findings tuple and is
not refused, so this is consistent with D17 and is not a gap to be filled with a third arm.
