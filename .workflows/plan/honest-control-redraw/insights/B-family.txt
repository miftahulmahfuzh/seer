Seven of the thirteen D17-blocked control arms traded far too MUCH -- 2.25x to 4.41x on trades and
1.99x to 5.20x on turnover (M0208-HASH-N20 at 4.41x/5.20x, M0243-NEAR20-R 3.01x/2.98x, M0201's two
seeds 2.26-2.27x, M0202's three 2.25x). The mechanism is the mirror image of family A: the draw
was re-keyed on the MONTH using m0187.draw(seed, symbol, data_date), so the whole control book was
redrawn at every decision while the real book's ranking persisted. M0243's own file records the
overshoot in prose -- "M0227's own placebo used a sticky hash order and traded 41 times in twenty
years ... this one is redrawn each month" -- so the author saw family A, corrected by hand, and
landed in family B. One sentence, two errors, and the lesson is that the re-key period must be
DERIVED from the book it controls, never chosen.

Pre-registered direction: an over-trading control over-pays its fees, so an honestly shaped
replacement earns MORE, so the book's lead NARROWS or REVERSES. Measured in trial_funding.mwr

Score: 2 of 7 arms and 2 of 4 methods moved in the pre-registered direction -- and the split by
handicap ratio is perfect: every arm at 2.25-2.27x widened, both arms at 3.01x and above narrowed.
The tempting reading is a single crossover where churn stops paying for itself somewhere between
2.27x and 3.01x. THAT READING WAS TESTED ACROSS ALL THIRTEEN ARMS AND IS NOT SUPPORTED. Three
things refute it. (1) Family A's signs contradict it four times out of six: under one curve, the
stickiest arm in the lab (M0227-R20 at 0.07x) should show the largest POSITIVE move of all and it
shows -1.318; and M0047's arms put the curve's peak below 0.55x while M0202's put it above 2.25x,
which no one curve can do. (2) The seed noise at a FIXED handicap is as large as the whole effect:
M0202's two seeds, same book, same guard, same 2.25x, differ by 1.55 points of move and M0201's by
2.11, against a between-group gap of 2.24. (3) It is a two-chassis-versus-two-chassis split, not
seven arms versus two; a random 2/2 assignment lands this way with probability 1/6.

What all thirteen arms DO support is not a direction but a collapse of variance. The handicapped
controls earned 4.381% to 11.381% a year (sd 1.918); the honest ones earn 6.047% to 9.532% (sd
0.988) -- 3.77x less variance. On the two chassis carrying two seeds, the handicapped seeds sit
2.190 and 1.737 points apart and the honest ones 0.079 and 0.187: 27.6x and 9.3x tighter. A
handicapped control is not a weaker or a stronger opponent. It is an ARBITRARY one, arbitrary to
within about seven points a year, which is larger than any margin in this set. That is why two
phases measuring faithfully reached opposite conclusions, and it is why there is no correction
factor for a margin quoted against a mis-shaped control -- only a re-draw.

M0208 was the arm most at risk: +5.87 pts was the lab's biggest quoted placebo margin and it was
quoted against an opponent trading 4.41x as often. It now reads +4.346 -- and that is PROVISIONAL
and a LOWER BOUND, because the honest control still trades only 0.73x, so a correctly-shaped one
would pay the rest of the fee bill and earn less than 9.532. The method's own 2-point success
condition stays PASS either way.

M0243 is the one that broke: its own written condition was "NEAR20 must beat its placebo by 2 or
more points a year", it passed at +6.068 against a 3.01x opponent, and against an honest one it
reads +1.619 and FAILS. Its analysis claim that the blind draw was "paying the same fees" is
measurably false and is withdrawn with it.
