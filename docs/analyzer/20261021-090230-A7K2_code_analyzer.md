## Detailed Requirements Understanding

**Problem statement.** `lab timing` (built 2026-10-21, commit `5be66c6`) introduced a column the
lab had never printed: the **de-funded** whole-window lead, deposits removed from both curves
before any return is read. Set beside the **funded** money-weighted lead already in
`trial_funding`, it exposed eight trials where the two disagree in sign. The handover reads that
disagreement as eight possible bad verdicts. The analysis below shows it is something else, and
that the something else is checkable.

**Success criteria.** Every claim stated here is a recomputation over recorded rows, reproducible
without a store and without a look. A superseding entry is written only where a later recorded
row makes an earlier one factually wrong.

**Key constraint.** `insights` is append-only; one column, `supersedes`, may move once from NULL.
Everything else is frozen, and the freeze is proved by digest before and after.

## Current Dataflow: the lab's two return columns

### Column 1 — funded (money-weighted)

**Produced at:** `backtest/benchmark.py` with `contributions`, recorded into `trials.cagr`,
`trials.spy_tr_cagr` (in IDR, on the owner's real deposit schedule) and `trial_funding.mwr` /
`trial_funding.spy_tr_mwr` (annualised money-weighted, in USD).

**Consumed by:** the condition `beats SPY TR`, and through it `trials.failed` and `trials.eligible`
— the lab's pass/fail screen. Also quoted in `methods.verdict` and in owner-facing journal entries.

### Column 2 — de-funded (price-only)

**Produced at:** `walkforward.measure(curve, start, end, deposits)` → `regime.defunded()`.

**Consumed by:** the walk-forward **fold majority** (a hard-gate promotion condition), `era_edge`
(the 2009–2015 positive-lead condition), and the reports `lab exposure`, `lab gate`,
`lab survivorship`, `lab shortfall` and `lab timing`'s `paper` column.

### Where they diverge

Each month's contribution to a lead is the difference of two de-funded log returns. The funded
figure weights those same months by the money in the account during them. The owner's deposits are
level, so the money-in line is straight: a lead earned early is weighted down and a lead earned
late is weighted up. **Sign disagreement is therefore a timing statement, not an error in either
column.**

Measured across all 761 funded dev trials:

| direction | count |
|---|---|
| leads on the prices, loses on the money | **8** |
| loses on the prices, wins on the money | **22** |
| total disagreement | 30 of 761 (one in 26) |

## Finding 1 — the eight reversals (R1)

Every one of the eight is a `dev` trial whose `failed` column already contains **`beats SPY TR`**.
The lab's screen reads the funded column, so it was never fooled by one of them.

| trial | candidate | paper | delivered | money-weighted return | SPY | method verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 670 | M0203-TF-N20-NOSW | +0.59 | **−0.46** | 6.729 | 7.188 | rejected |
| 519 | M0125-DV-C10 | +0.54 | **−0.28** | 6.904 | 7.188 | rejected |
| 302 | M0080-EX-S200-T | +0.54 | **−0.25** | 6.933 | 7.188 | rejected |
| 396 | M0130-P20-GATE | +0.87 | **−0.17** | 7.015 | 7.188 | rejected |
| 849 | M0201-Y20-MV10-PLC2 | +0.85 | **−0.12** | 7.069 | 7.188 | dev-eligible (placebo arm) |
| 554 | M0172-LO5-P100-N20-T | +0.73 | **−0.10** | 7.089 | 7.188 | rejected |
| 736 | M0222-TILT2-T | +1.07 | **−0.05** | 7.135 | 7.188 | rejected |
| 848 | M0201-Y20-MV10-PLC1 | +0.71 | **−0.04** | 7.148 | 7.188 | dev-eligible (placebo arm) |

**Each verdict was then checked against the recorded money-weighted figures, claim by claim.**
Every one holds, and several quote `trial_funding.mwr` exactly:

- **M0203** — "beat SPY on the deposits (+10.4% vs +7.2% a year at best)". `M0203-TF-N40` mwr =
  **10.404**, SPY = **7.188**. Exact. "beat a meaningless-order placebo by less than the 2 points
  promised": the size-matched pairing is `TF-N20` (9.698) against `HASH-N20` (7.882) = **+1.82**,
  under the pre-registered 2. Correct. The reversal arm is not the arm the verdict is about.
- **M0125** — "bought back part of the shortfall to SPY … deepened the worst fall from 57% to as
  much as 73%". `EQ` 6.456 → `DV-C10` 6.904; shortfall narrows from −0.73 to −0.28. Correct.
- **M0080** — "matched SPY at best". The book arm `ANN-S200-T` is 7.281 against 7.188. Correct.
  Insight 158's "the cutters themselves earned 5.3% where SPY earned 7.2%" is `ANN-S200` = 5.312.
  Exact.
- **M0130** — "picked worse than simply holding the most-traded big companies": `P20` 2.200
  against the control `DV20` 5.572. Correct. Insight 217 goes further and quotes the reversal arm
  itself: "behind the usual market switch it nearly matched the S&P 500 (7.0% a year against 7.2%
  on the same deposits)" — `P20-GATE` mwr = **7.015** against **7.188**. The one note in the
  journal that names a reversal arm directly records it, correctly, as a loss.
- **M0172** — "barely ahead of SPY over twenty years": headline `LO5-P150-N20-T` 7.916 vs 7.188.
  Correct. Insight 312's "it got worse" on the tighter pool: `LO5-P100-N20-T` 7.089 < 7.916.
  Correct.
- **M0222** — "earned +6.3% a year against SPY's +7.2%" (`TILT1` = 6.318, exact); "the raise lean
  beat a random lean by a few tenths" (+0.23 and +0.45, exact); "the top-50 core trails SPY by more
  than a point" (`CORE` 5.963, −1.23). All correct.
- **M0201** — "beat random picks of the same shape by about 2.4 points a year": 9.543 − 7.148 =
  **+2.395** and 9.543 − 7.069 = **+2.474**. Exact. The two reversal arms *are* the placebos; a
  placebo losing to the market is the result, not a mistake.

**Conclusion on R1: zero superseding entries are warranted on the ground the handover named.** No
verdict reads as a positive result on a trial that lost the owner money. The lab's verdicts were
written on the funded column from the start; the de-funded column is the newcomer.

### What the read-through did find

Following M0201 — one of the eight — into the journal surfaced a genuine wrong row that nobody
had threaded. **Insight 397** (2026-10-17) judges the size-weighted bond-like book against two
random twins earning "8.1% and 10.3%". Those are `M0201-PLC2-MV10` (8.065) and `M0201-PLC1-MV10`
(10.255) — control arms the lab **formally retired three days later**. Insight 472 measured them
at 2.26x and 2.27x the real book's trade rate and concluded a mis-shaped control "is not a weaker
or a stronger opponent, it is an ARBITRARY one, arbitrary to within about seven points a year";
insight 473 gave D17 a supersession clause and the arms were re-drawn as trials 848/849. Insight
472 records the replacement margins: **−0.712 → +2.395** (sign flipped) and **+1.479 → +2.475**.

M0201's `methods.verdict` was corrected at the time. **Insight 397 was not**, and it was the only
place in the journal still carrying the retired reading — stating, in its title, the opposite of
what the method's own verdict now says. That is the supersede test exactly: a later recorded row
makes an earlier one factually wrong.

## Finding 2 — the four methods with M0267's shape (R2)

`lab timing <trial>` against the default `REF-SPY-HOLD`:

| trial | paper | delivered | delivered / paper | 1996–2002 share | half-lead at |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M0054-ALWAYS-IN | +2.35 | +1.05 | 44.5% | +116.3% | 20.9% |
| M0054-PARK-SPY | +3.53 | +2.55 | 72.1% | +76.6% | 21.8% |
| M0176-BIG | +4.60 | +3.15 | 68.5% | +92.5% | 21.8% |
| M0110-DP20-UP | +3.83 | +3.10 | 80.7% | +79.4% | 21.8% |
| M0201-Y20-MV10-PLC2 | +0.85 | −0.12 | −13.9% | +220.8% | 20.5% |

All four earn the bulk of their lead in 1996–2002, at 36% of deposits. **None is as extreme as
M0267's 21%**, and four of the five still delivered 44–81% of their paper lead. These are
front-loaded books, not broken ones.

The important question is *which ingredient* is front-loaded — and the measurement separates it.

## Finding 3 — the gate is not the front-loaded ingredient (R3)

`lab timing <gate arm> --against <no-gate sibling>` isolates the safety rule by holding the book
fixed. Seven pairs exist in the record:

| pair (gate vs its own no-gate twin) | paper | delivered | delivered / paper | 2008's share of lead | half-lead at |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M0030-SAT100 vs -NOGATE | +3.10 | **+4.01** | **129.4%** | +128.8% | 65.3% |
| M0054-PARK-SPY vs -ALWAYS-IN | +1.18 | **+1.50** | **127.2%** | +52.0% | 25.1% |
| M0203-TF-N20 vs -NOSW | +2.37 | **+2.97** | **125.4%** | +175.6% | 64.4% |
| M0130-P20-GATE vs -P20 | +4.08 | **+4.81** | **118.0%** | +99.6% | 34.7% |
| M0080-ANN-S200-T vs -ANN-S200 | +1.92 | **+1.97** | **102.7%** | +129.8% | 33.9% |
| M0222-TILT2-T vs -TILT2 | +1.38 | +0.99 | 72.3% | +142.5% | 33.5% |
| M0201-Y20-MV10-SW vs -Y20-MV10 | −0.42 | −0.35 | (lead is negative) | — | — |

**Six of seven delivered 103%–129% of their paper value.** The gate's payday for a book that steps
into cash is **2008**, by which time **66.1%** of the owner's deposits had arrived — not 2000–2002
at 36%.

The decomposition is visible inside single methods:

| method | picking alone (no-gate arm vs SPY) | the gate alone (gate arm vs no-gate arm) |
|---|---|---|
| M0203 | +0.59 paper → **−0.46** delivered (−78%) | +2.37 paper → **+2.97** delivered (125%) |
| M0054 | +2.35 paper → +1.05 delivered (44.5%) | +1.18 paper → **+1.50** delivered (127%) |

**The front-loading belongs to the stock picking, not to the safety rule.** The picks' payday is
the dot-com run-up, when a third of the money had arrived; the gate's is 2008, when two thirds had.

### Why M0267 reads the other way

M0267's gate never leaves the market — it steps sideways into the safer of two equity funds. Both
funds fell together in 2008, so 2008 paid it nothing, and the only crash that separated them was
the early one. **M0267's 21% delivered fraction is a property of its chassis, not of the gate.**

## Finding 4 — the fold majority, the one place the de-funded column gates a decision

`walkforward.measure` de-funds before it slices (`walkforward.py:135-139`), so the **fold
majority** — a hard-gate promotion condition — is scored on the price column, not the money one.
In principle a method could win its majority on prices while losing the owner money.

Checked across every method that has ever won one. There are **15**, and the version each won on
beat SPY on the owner's money by **+1.4 to +11.8 points a year**. The gap is real in principle and
has **never once opened in practice**. It needs no fix today and is worth re-checking whenever that
test changes.

## Finding 5 — the handover misnumbers the gate-premium entry (R3)

**Insight 494 is not about the gate.** It is "A rule nobody chose was leaving a tenth of every wide
book sitting in cash" — the resize band, written 2026-10-20 against M0127. Nothing in it concerns
the safety rule or a 3.6 pts/yr premium, and nothing in this re-read touches it.

The gate-premium entry is **insight 463**, "Across all 824 recorded trials, most of the lab's
post-2009 shortfall is the safety rule standing in cash". It is the only row in the journal that
mentions 824 trials.

**And 463's 3.6 is not a lump-sum number.** Its derivation is explicit: "The market rose 16.6% a
year over those six and a half years, so being a fifth out of it costs 3.6 points" — an annualised
rate over **2009–2015**, the era in which **100% of the owner's deposits have arrived**. Far from
being "largely fictional for a monthly saver", it is the most money-faithful headline in the
journal: it is the one era where the funded and de-funded weightings coincide exactly.

The handover's premise is therefore inverted. The correct asymmetry is sharper and worse-sounding
than the one it states, and it runs the other way:

- **the gate's cost** sits after 2009, on 100% of the money → **fully real, not discounted**
- **the gate's payout** sits in 2008, on 66% of the money → still largely real, and on six of
  seven pairs the money received **more** than the prices promised
