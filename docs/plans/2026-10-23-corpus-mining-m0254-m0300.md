## 2. The meta-patterns

### P1 — Most ladders in this range could not have measured their own dial

M0296 ran **18 blind draws of one rule**: CAGR spread **5.50 pts**, worst-fall spread **20.7 pts**.
M0297 ran 6 draws of a stock-screen shape: **5.64 pts** and **29.4 pts**. Those are the widths of
*nothing happening*.

**25 of the 29 ran methods have a whole-ladder CAGR spread below 5.50 pts.** The dial moved the
result less than redrawing the same rule does.

| spread | methods |
|---|---|
| under 2 pts | M0284 (0.66), M0266 (0.87), M0281 (0.93), M0275 (1.03), M0276 (1.21), M0270 (1.37), M0283 (1.47), M0262 (1.66), M0295 (1.96) |
| 2–4 pts | M0300, M0292, M0287, M0267, M0261, M0254, M0277, M0298, M0271, M0279 |
| 4–5.5 pts | M0265, M0286, M0293, M0288, M0258, M0260 |
| above the floor | M0278 (5.63), M0297 (5.64), M0282 (6.27) |

**The caveat that keeps this honest:** draw noise is a property of the chassis, not a constant.
M0296's draws sit on a gate-off book with falls of 0.49–0.70; M0288 and M0298 sit on a
volume-floored gated book whose falls span only **0.200–0.230**. A quiet chassis plausibly has a
much narrower error bar, which is exactly why those two ladders may be readable at 3.85 and 4.66.
**That number has never been measured on the quiet chassis** — and until it is, no ladder on it
can be called readable either. That is candidate C1.

### P2 — "dev-eligible" has come apart from "found something"

`stock-event-drift` is 4 for 4 eligible, and three of the four verdicts say the same thing:

- M0277: *"only the unchanged parent book passed, so nothing new to promote"*
- M0279: *"every filtered book lost to the unfiltered one; only the unchanged parent book passed"*
- M0284: *"widening the news-day pool was the wrong question"*

The eligibility belongs to the **chassis**, not to the experiment. Counting eligible arms as hits
overstates the discovery rate of this range by most of its apparent value.

### P3 — `portfolio-rebalancing` is closed by arithmetic, 5 for 5

Five ran, none eligible, and the verdicts converge rather than scatter:

- M0266 — *"the rebalancing was never the point, the cash was"*
- M0270 — *"the line is straight … what is not constant is the slope, 0.005 to 0.173 a year between eras"*
- M0286 — *"a book shaped like the market re-weights itself"*
- M0292 — *"a straight line with no best setting"*
- M0300 — *"the exposure effect M0292 could not separate was zero"*

A size-weighted book re-weights itself by arithmetic. Cadence, band, and weighting-power are not
dials on it. Three ideas remain queued in this family (M0269, M0272, M0274) and two restate
settled questions.

### P4 — the trend-switch dial is exhausted, with seven ideas still queued behind it

Six ran: cadence (M0260), reading phase (M0261), rarity (M0262), price band (M0258), graded vote
(M0287), premium-in-shares (M0267), time-in-cash decomposition (M0271). The axis turns **at**
monthly and falls away on both sides. Queued: M0255, M0259, M0264, M0273, M0290, M0291 — six more
cuts of one dial.

### P5 — what has actually worked is the pool, not the weights or the clock

The two shallowest-falling eligible books in the whole range are the liveness pair: M0288 and
M0298, falls **0.200–0.230**, against 0.33–0.82 for the volatility and trend families. Both change
**which names are eligible to be picked**. Every measured-dead dial in this range changes either
**how much weight** a name gets (M0266, M0270, M0286, M0292, M0300) or **when the book is read**
(M0260–M0264, M0267, M0287).

**The corpus has exhausted weight dials and clock dials and has barely touched pool dials.** That
is where the next twenty candidates should come from, and it is the single structural fact this
mining produced.

### Why the cuts are cuts

Seven are already in the backlog (C6, C7, C9, C10, C11, C13, C14) — proposing them is proposing
work the lab has already queued, and the dedupe table holds 504 keys precisely so this does not
happen. Five restate a measured result rather than testing one (C8, C12, C15, C16, C17): the dial
has been turned and the answer recorded, so a new id buys a second copy of a known number. Three
run against a known-bad instrument (C18's broken figure, C19's measured-worse universe, C20's
non-tradeable arm). One is a near-duplicate of its own parent (C5).

**The honest cost of harshness:** the lab holds **122 open ideas**. Generating twenty more and
keeping three is the correct ratio only because the corpus is mature; a younger lab would keep more.

## 4. The result that matters: all four survivors were already queued

Before claiming novelty, the four survivors were checked against the 504 dedupe keys and the
backlog. **Every one of them already exists as a queued idea above M0300**, and in each case the
queued version is strictly more specific than the one this mining derived:

| Derived here | Already queued as | The queued version knows more |
|---|---|---|
| C1 — noise band on the quiet chassis | **M0306** | names the exact gap: *"every one of those nineteen was ALWAYS INVESTED, and that is not the chassis the lab actually uses"* |
| C1's interpretation half | **M0307** | already has the number — 2.5 pts/yr noise scale for a two-arm gap, and the p = 1/(k+1) ceiling |
| C2 — proximity to high, survivorship-positive | **M0303** | knows it is one of only **two** such rules and names the second, with a monotone ladder as evidence |
| C3 — a pick filter with no cash rule to mask it | **M0302** | has the measurement: 2008 goes −39.5% → −24.5% with the switch off, 4 of those 15 points beyond the best of four blind draws |

Twelve ideas sit above M0300 (M0301–M0312) and the dedupe table holds 504 keys. Independent
derivation from the corpus converged on the queued set — which is **evidence the backlog is
well-formed, and evidence that generating more ideas is the wrong intervention.**

**The lab is not idea-starved. It is prioritisation-starved.** 122 open ideas, four runnable
candidates derivable from the corpus's own structure, and all four already written down. A
generator that emits twenty more adds to a pile of 122; what the corpus asks for is an ordering
over what is already there.

So the deliverable inverts: not five new pre-registration documents, but a **ranked dispatch
order over existing ideas**, which `/explore-and-experiment-new-method --method MNNNN` consumes
with no editing at all — a stricter reading of "dispatch without editing" than a new file could
satisfy.

## 5. The dispatch order

Ranked by expected information gain. The first two are measurement infrastructure the other three
depend on for readability — that is a dependency claim, not a preference.

1. **M0307 — re-read every recorded single-control comparison against the measured coin width.**

3. **M0302 — can a calm filter replace the cash rule instead of sitting behind it?** The only

5. **M0309 — is the illiquidity book buying an anomaly or buying bad data?** Squarely in §2's live
   zone: a pool filter on the chassis with the shallowest falls in the corpus.

## 6. What this does not claim

- The 5.50-pt floor is M0296's chassis, not a universal constant. §2's table is a flag, not a
  verdict — and resolving that is precisely what M0306 and M0307 are for.
- `dev-eligible` counts are from `trials.eligible`; only the verdict *text* distinguishes a chassis
  pass from a dial finding, and the text was read for the four event-drift methods only.
- The convergence in §4 is not proof the backlog is complete. It shows this corpus's structure
  points at ideas already written down; a different mining axis might point elsewhere.
- No new method id is justified by this document.
