## Detailed Requirements Understanding

**Requirement statement.** Pre-register and run one lab method, `M0258`, whose allocator holds an
existing stock book and replaces the shared `close > SMA200` gate with a symmetric price band —
out below `SMA200 × (1 − b)`, back in above `SMA200 × (1 + b)` — and whose arms let the band be
read as a paired difference against the same book's bare gate, across a ladder of `b`.

**Success criteria.** The method file is pure, passes the lab contract tests, is committed (the
commit *is* the pre-registration), runs through `lab run M0258`, and leaves an honest record:
analysis, verdict, at least one journalled insight, at least one queued next idea, and a staged
`web/data/lab.json`. **Clearing the dev screen is not the success criterion** — this is a
measurement, and a null is a result. No test-window look is spent.

**Key considerations, and the two that change the design.**

1. **The lab has already run this experiment twice, and both times the band lost.** The handover
   never quotes the numbers. They are in the committed database:

   Quoted in **money** — the money-weighted return on the owner's monthly top-ups, from
   `trial_funding.mwr`, because these books are funded and `trials.total_return` and `cagr` count
   the deposits as gains (`metrics.py`: *"deliberately left as they are ... they are not returns at
   all"*; memory `seer-funded-book-cagr-is-a-lie`).

   `M0101` is a band on a book that holds stocks, with a paired bare-gate control, at `b` = 3% and
   5%. It is R1 + R2 + half of R3, **already recorded**, and the band earned less and fell further:
   −0.18 pts/yr at 3%, −0.52 at 5%, and 1.68 points deeper in both cases. `m0101.band_on` is the
   band implementation, and `M0084` reuses it verbatim.

   **And the lab has already recorded the difference-in-differences this plan proposes to measure,
   on SPY alone.** The band minus the bare gate, in funded points a year and points of worst fall:

   | gate read | band − gate, money | band − gate, worst fall |
   |---|---|---|
   | monthly | **−0.40 pts/yr** | **1.80 deeper** |
   | weekly | **+3.42 pts/yr** | **14.22 shallower** |

   A **3.82-point swing** in what the band is worth, driven by nothing but how often the gate is
   read. What is untested is whether it survives a book that holds stocks — which is R1's gap,
   and the method's question.

   Note also: the band did **not** cut turnover on the stock book (4.73 vs 4.78) or on the monthly
   SPY book (0.826 vs 0.826). R4's premise — *"the band cuts turnover sharply"* — is true only of
   the weekly SPY arm (0.86 vs 3.38). On a monthly stock book the gate is a few flips against a
   thousand-trade book, so it moves the shape by under 1%.

2. **The handover's headline compares against a baseline the lab does not run.** Its study acts on
   SPY **daily**; every lab book reads its gate at its own rank session, which is **monthly**.
   Measured for this analysis on the dev store (`docs/backtests/2026-10-19-gate-band-cadence.py`,
   written by the plan's phase):

   The band's worth is a **monotone function of how often the gate is read**, and it reaches zero
   at the cadence the lab actually runs:

   | gate read | band 3% − bare gate, return | band 3% − bare gate, drawdown |
   |---|---|---|
   | daily | **+1.82 pts/yr** | **+8.4 pts better** |
   | weekly | **+2.52 pts/yr** | **+15.6 pts better** |
   | monthly | **−0.12 pts/yr** | **1.7 pts worse** |

   The monthly bare gate beats band-3%-daily on *every* axis in the handover's own table — return
   (9.08 vs 7.87), drawdown (−19.0 vs −20.3) and the 2009-2015 premium (10.07 vs 8.83).

   This grid independently reproduces both recorded lab results, which is why it is trusted here:
   `M0084-MONTHLY`'s recorded 18.9% fall against this grid's monthly bare-gate −19.0%, and
   `M0084-BAND3-M`'s recorded 20.7% against this grid's monthly band-3% −20.7%, to the digit; and
   the weekly pair reproduces `M0084-WEEKLY` (215.3% return, 33.1% fall) against `M0084-BAND3-W`
   (330.3%, 18.9%) in both sign and magnitude.

   **The mechanism is not new, it is the same one.** A band suppresses flips shorter than the band
   is wide; a monthly reading suppresses flips shorter than a month. The handover's own numbers say
   so: its band-3% leaves "exactly 11 episodes — the number insight 452 reports", and insight 452
   counted the gate **at its monthly checks**. The handover reads that coincidence as "the lab has
   been reasoning about a cleaner gate than the one it actually runs". It is the other way round:
   the monthly reading *is* the cleaner gate, and it is already cleaner than the band.

   This does not kill the method. It changes what the method is for: not "does the band win" — the
   lab has answered that twice — but **"is the band's worth a function of the gate's reading
   cadence, on a book that holds stocks?"** That question is open, has a sharp pre-registrable
   prediction, and is worth one method id. The answer either way is a correction the lab needs.

3. **R4's premise is factually wrong, and the right control is better than the one asked for.**
   Verified against `lab/hardgate.py` (D17) and `lab/control_shape.py`, and by running
   `lab control M0101 M0084 M0245 M0197`:
   - D17 **does not refuse a method with no control arm** — "most of the lab has none, and the rule
     makes no claim about a book nobody drew a control for" (hardgate.py, D17, *What it does not do*).
   - D17 shape-checks only arms whose one-line `rationale` matches a `DRAW_MARKER`
     (`"placebo"`, `"hash-drawn"`, `"hash order"`). A **dose-response** arm — matched by
     `DOSE_MARKERS`, tested *before* the draw markers — carries verdict `not-checked`, holds no
     ratio, and the rule never reads it.
   - `lab control M0101 M0084 M0245 M0197` reports exactly one row across all four gate methods:
     `M0245-FALL63`, classified `dose-response`, `not-checked`. None of the four has ever been near
     a D17 refusal.
   - A hash-timed placebo is **mis-shaped by construction** on a monthly stock book. A random
     monthly switch at the gate's own ~31% out-rate flips roughly four times a year against the
     gate's ~0.5; on a 20-name book each flip is ~40 trades, so it would land near 7× the arm it
     controls — far outside `REFUSE_BAND = (0.5, 2.0)`. A placebo that *matches* the band's realised
     episode count and lengths needs the band's whole realised path, which is look-ahead.

   So the correct control here is the ladder itself, with `b = 0` as the paired bare gate. That is
   the lab's own standing finding (memory `seer-dose-response-beats-a-placebo`: "more SPY gives less
   lead, so a frame effect is excluded by construction... treat a single placebo arm as
   corroboration, never as the load") and it satisfies R4's *intent* — a control that is not
   handicapped — better than the arm R4 names.

4. **`b = 0` reduces exactly to the lab's gate.** `m0101.band_on` scans back for the newest session
   whose `close/SMA200` ratio is outside `1 ± b`; at `b = 0` the newest such session is the current
   one, so it returns `close > SMA200` — `f_factor.trend_on`. The `b = 0` rung is therefore both
   the ladder's zero dose and an exact machinery reproduction of the host book's recorded trial.

5. **The host must be kin-clean, promotable, and cadence-robust.** D14 behavioural kin refuses a
   method whose book moves with a `test-failed` book at ≥0.85 residual correlation; the four
   test-failed methods are M0002, M0021, M0022, M0029. Three methods read "Promotable now" and
   kin-clean: `M0072-Z2-RAWRANK`, `M0092-SQRT-CAP10`, `M0197-Y20-SW`. Only **M0072**'s engine is
   cadence-robust — its event hold is counted in sessions, not calendar months, and its parent
   M0063 ran the identical book weekly (`M0063-N20-TREND`) and monthly (`M0063-M-N20-TREND`).
   M0197's chooser keys its re-pick on `data_date.month in (3,6,9,12)`, so a weekly cadence would
   re-rank it every week of a re-pick month: it cannot carry the cadence axis.

**Assumptions, stated.**
- The dev screen is `DEV_MAX_DRAWDOWN = 0.50`; the real-money bar `MAX_DRAWDOWN = 0.25` is not this
  method's bar and is not collapsed into it.
- `weekly-hold-frac-gotrade` is M0063's one-off rule set, **not** in `sim.rules.PRESETS`, so a
  weekly arm can never be promoted. The weekly arms are diagnostic by design; the promotable arm is
  monthly. This is stated in the method file rather than worked around.
- It differs from `monthly-hold-frac-gotrade` in exactly two fields — `cadence` and `id` (verified
  by dataclass diff) — so the cadence axis is clean.

### Processing chain inside a candidate

1. **`Allocator.lookback(params)`** → bars of run-up. Caps the dev window's first scored session.
   `M0072` returns `max(jump_lookback(p.jump), p.jump.hold + p.sd_base + 2)`; its recorded dev
   window starts **1996-01-03**. An overlay that does not widen this keeps the identical window,
   which is what makes the `b = 0` arm a reproduction rather than an approximation.
2. **`Allocator.prepare(history)`** → `ZPrepared(history, prepare_factor(history))`, the F4 columns
   plus a per-symbol event/sigma cache.
3. **`targets_prepared(prepared, members, data_date, held, params)`** at each rank session:
   - `trend_on(prepared.history, data_date, p.jump.inner)` — **this is the single line the overlay
     replaces**. False ⇒ `return ()` ⇒ the whole book goes to cash.
   - `prepared.factor.rows_on(members, data_date, p.jump.inner)` — F4's $5 / $20M screen
   - `_targets(...)` — live events, 20 slots at 5%, held names first, remainder parked in SPY
4. **`sim`** turns targets into fills at Gotrade's real fees on the owner's monthly top-ups.

### The band, as the lab already implements it

**Location:** `engine/src/seer_engine/lab/methods/m0101_calm_raisers_banded_switch.py:103`

```python
def band_on(h: History | None, data_date: date, n: int, band: float) -> bool:
    """Hysteresis on close / SMA(n): off below 1-band, on above 1+band, else the last crossing's side."""
```

- scans back at most `SCAN = 504` sessions from `data_date`
- the newest session with `r < 1 − band` reads **off**; the newest with `r > 1 + band` reads **on**;
  whichever is newer wins
- no crossing within `SCAN` falls back to the strict `r[-1] > 1.0`
- `lookback` deliberately does **not** add `SCAN` (M0101's docstring: that would move the window off
  the parent's and break the paired control)
- state is read on **daily** closes; the book acts on it only at its own rank session

**At `band = 0` it is `trend_on`**: the first ratio examined is the current session's, which is
either `< 1` or `> 1`.
