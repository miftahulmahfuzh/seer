## 1. What this project is, in four sentences

Seer is a trading-method laboratory. Ideas become `MNNNN` methods, run once on a **dev window**
(1996-01-03 .. 2015-10-16), and the good ones get exactly **one** look at a held-out **test
window** (2015-10-19 .. today) before going on a paper roster. The owner mirrors that paper roster
with his own money, so a bad promotion is not an academic error. The lab's discipline is
pre-registration: write down what you will measure, in git, before you measure it.

## 2. The thing that happened today, and why everything else follows from it

The owner asked a curiosity question — *what would my money be now if I had started investing when
I got my first salary in April 2018?* Answering it honestly required building
`/calculate-assets`, and the answer was bad:

| | Ending value | A year, money-weighted |
|---|---|---|
| Paid in (10jt + 5jt × 102) | Rp 520,000,000 | — |
| His book (M0032) | Rp 873,501,015 | +11.7% |
| **The same deposits in SPY** | **Rp 1,288,548,261** | **+20.4%** |

Then the audit: **all four roster strategies lost to a deposit-matched SPY**, every one of them
falling 29-34% against a 20% rule. Then the formal looks: `M0022-W-TV14` and `M0002-REL-85` both
`test-failed`. **Five out-of-sample results, five failures**, all on `beats SPY TR`. The deflated
Sharpe did not survive either — M0022 went 0.912 on dev to 0.136 on test.

Root cause: every roster strategy was admitted by `owner-override` while the lab's own status read
`rejected`, and no test-window store existed until this morning, so the dev leaderboard had been
the only referee the lab ever had.

**Two diagnoses were proposed and both were wrong.** They are written down because a cold session
will propose them again:

- *"It is a market-breadth regime bet."* `lab regime` was built to test this. It does **not**
  discriminate: on month-by-month breadth labels almost every method reads "pays in both",
  M0007-N20-RAW included, at +6.6% narrow / +7.7% broad — and it went on to lose. What separates
  the eras is how long narrowness *lasts*, not how deep it gets, and the dev window contains no
  sustained spell at anything like 2023-2026's strength (6 of 202 three-year windows positive,
  peak +0.17%/month, against +0.68% ending December 2025). **No dev statistic could have predicted
  it.** (`regime.persistence`, insight 58.)
- *"Walk-forward would have caught it."* Partly. It flags three of the four recorded test
  failures (M0021, M0022, M0002 each won 2 of 4 folds) and misses M0029 (3 of 4). It does **not**
  flag M0007, the family that cost real money. (insight 76.)

## 5. The open work

### 5.1 PRIMARY TARGET — recorded trials are not reproducible, and the lab compares across stores

**The evidence, all measured today:**

- `lab costs M0011` re-ran `M0011-RAW20-TV14-N21` at the flat cost and got **+545.3%** where trial
  #90 records **+660.2%**. The command prints its own warning: *"compare the two re-runs with each
  other, not with the recorded trial."*
- The lab's 152 trials were recorded under **three different store fingerprints**:

  | fingerprint | dev trials | recorded |
  |---|---|---|
  | `399d0d254c7a` (current) | 84 | Oct 6-9 |
  | `5451195fd552` | 58 | Oct 4 |
  | `e597367bb680` | 6 | Oct 5 |

- **`REF-SPY-HOLD`, the benchmark every walk-forward fold is scored against, lives in
  `5451195fd552`** — not the current store. Rebuilt from the current store the same curve ends
  **+1.83%** higher, with a worst monthly gap of 1.90%.

**Why it is not a fire today, and why it will be.** Measured: of **130** scoreable fold verdicts
across the lab, only **3** sit inside that 1.9% drift — M0030 fold 1 (−0.32 pts), M0021 fold 1
(−0.36), H-P7A-F1 fold 4 (−0.91) — and every one of the three is blocked by another condition
anyway, so **no gate outcome changes**. Today's verdicts stand. But the hard gate now makes real
promotion decisions out of cross-store arithmetic, and the margin only has to be thin once.

The root cause is known and documented in the repo already: yfinance answers differently from one
day to the next, so a rebuild is never bit-identical, which is exactly why
`.claude/skills/sync-research-store/` exists — *copying* a store keeps one fingerprint and
rebuilding does not.

**Questions to answer with measurement, not assumption** (the lab-hard-gate set did this well on
2026-10-09; copy its approach — state the options, measure what each costs, pick, and say why):

1. **What is the policy?** Candidates, not a menu to pick blind: (a) pin the dev store forever and
   make a rebuild an explicit, versioned event; (b) re-run the benchmark under the current store so
   at least the yardstick matches the majority; (c) make the gate refuse a comparison whose two
   sides carry different `store_fingerprint`, failing closed; (d) record enough per trial to
   re-derive. Measure how many trials and verdicts each would strand.
2. **Is `trials.store_fingerprint` enough to detect it?** It is already recorded on every row.
   Should `lab walkforward`, `lab regime` and `hardgate` warn — or refuse — on a mismatch?
3. **How far does the drift reach?** 1.83% was measured on SPY buy-and-hold only. Quantify it for
   a method curve, not just the benchmark, before deciding (a)-(d).
4. **Does the 58-trial `5451195fd552` cohort need re-running, or marking?** `trials` is
   append-only by trigger, so "fixing" old rows is not available; only adding or annotating is.

**Hard constraints.** `trials` is append-only — triggers refuse UPDATE and DELETE. `test-failed`
and `test-passed` are final; `TRANSITIONS` has no edge out of either. Do not invent one.

### 5.2 The market-cap figure is wrong, and it leaks look-ahead

`f_fundamental` computes market cap as `snapshot.shares_outstanding * close(d)`. The share count
is the real unadjusted number from the filing; the close is **back-adjusted for splits that happen
after `d`**. Measured 2026-10-09: AAPL on 2014-06-30 reads **$20.0B** against a real ~$560B (the
2020 4:1 split), while MSFT and XOM read correctly because they never split again. **The error is
zero for some names and 28× for others, and it depends on future corporate actions** — so a
cross-sectional rank on it leaks look-ahead. It is the divisor of M0005's value leg, the lab's
worst family at MAR 0.02. Blocks **M0047**, which is queued waiting on it. Insight 69.

Note `FactorRow.dollar_volume` (close × volume) is **not** affected — volume is adjusted in step
with price — and M0034 used 20-day mean dollar volume as a size proxy instead, measuring Spearman
0.686 against market value. A cousin, not a substitute.

### 5.4 Four free datasets, three already queued as blocked-data ideas

Insight 59 has the full reasoning. **M0038** FRED (`T10Y2Y`, `BAA10Y`, `DGS10`, `DFII10`) — an
exogenous regime gate, where today every method shares one 200-day trend gate that is a function
of the price it is meant to protect against. **M0039** CBOE VIX/VIX3M — a term-structure gate that
inverts at the *start* of a shock, which is the shape of M0007's worst fall (the six weeks after
June 1998, not 2008 or 2000). **M0040** FINRA short interest — crowding, the documented mechanism
behind momentum crashes, in a lab with eleven flavours of momentum and no crowding measure. And
**RSP**, the equal-weight S&P, which is not yet an idea and should be: every verdict today turns on
`beats SPY TR`, which silently asks two questions at once — *is the stock-picking good* and *does
equal-weighting beat cap-weighting this decade* — and answers them with one number.

### 5.5 The one purchase, and when

**Do not buy survivorship-free price history until the buy signal fires.** Its four conditions are
in `lab/walkforward.py:BUY_CONDITIONS`, enforced in the explore skill's Promotion **step 0b**, and
answered by Sera every batch. Today the answer is **no buy signal**. The reasoning is in §5 of
`WALK_FORWARD_EVALUATION_PLAN.md`; the short version is that the dev store prices 48% of index
members in 1996 and 74% in 2014, the 522 it cannot price are disproportionately the companies that
died (AABA, AAMRQ), and a method whose edge dies by 2009-2015 can be rejected for free.

## 7. Traps that will bite a cold session

1. **Two modules are called `walkforward`.** `backtest/walkforward.py` is P3b's anchored yearly
   walk-forward for Strategy A2 on the bracket engine. `lab/walkforward.py` is the lab one. I
   overwrote the wrong one today and destroyed 32 tests with it; both were restored and verified
   byte-identical. **Check whether a file exists before writing it.**
2. **A funded curve must be de-funded before it is measured** (`regime.defunded`). Every trial
   from M0032 on is funded with the owner's 5,000,000 IDR a month, and a raw funded curve reads
   those deposits as growth — it did, by seventy to eighty points a year, until it was fixed.
   Insights 72 and 75.
3. **Never report CAGR on a funded book.** It counts the owner's deposits as profit: a funded dev
   trial records `cagr` 0.376 beside an `mwr` of 0.137. Use the money-weighted return, and take it
   over the **rupiah** when the answer is a rupiah balance.
4. **`lab` commands migrate `lab.sqlite` on connect — even a read dirties it.** Read it with
   `file:lab/lab.sqlite?mode=ro` when you only want to look. Only commit it through `lab stage`,
   which also regenerates `web/data/lab.json`; CI fails a database commit without it.
5. **Pushing `main` redeploys seertrade.site.** Vercel builds only `main` and the `/sera` pages are
   built from `web/data/lab.json`.
6. **Backticks execute inside double-quoted shell strings.** Two commit messages today lost text
   this way. Use a heredoc or a file for commit messages with code in them.
7. **The fundamentals panel starts in 2009**, not 2006. Zero rows filed before 2009 — the SEC only
   mandated XBRL then. Any panel-gated method holds nothing for 1996-2008, measures the filing
   mandate rather than the idea, and **burns the method id permanently** because
   `runner.preflight` refuses a second run.
8. **Sera may be running.** If `tmux list-windows` shows a `sera-*` or `orch-*` window, she owns
   `lab/lab.sqlite` commits and pushes to `main` after every child. Commit by pathspec, never
   `git add -A`, and never `reset --hard`.
