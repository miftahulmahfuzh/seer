## The three results worth carrying forward

**1. The five-dollar price floor reads the adjusted close, and repairing it is free.**
Worth +1.05 pts/yr money-weighted on dev, +0.87 on the survivorship-check store; a matched blind
draw from the same pools moves only +0.10, so the gain is the picking, not the pool. The honest
floor recovers 97% of what deleting the floor entirely recovers.
**The repair is SIX code sites, not the five the child reported** (insight 773, and appended to
M0387's analysis): `strategies/f_factor.py:190`, `strategies/f_fundamental.py:403`,
`methods/m0052_turn_of_month_tilt.py:145`, `methods/m0109_book_drawdown_brake.py:213`,
`methods/m0117_index_tenure_exclusion.py:200`, `methods/m0276_cheap_financial_health.py:294`.
The last keeps its own `MIN_PRICE = 5.0` at line 108 instead of taking the shared parameter, so a
sweep of the shared screen misses it. Already correct on the printed close: `m0383:223`,
`m0387:186` and `:188`. Three further modules (`m0119:147`, `m0127:165`, `m0266:214`) name 5.0 but
delegate the comparison to m0109 and need no change.
**This is the highest-value unlanded item in the lab.** It is measured, needs no new data, and may
change how older results read.

**2. We have been under-charging stacked safety rules by about half.** De-funded bills against the
never-step-aside arm: switch alone 2.90 pts/yr, fear-spike brake alone 5.04, term-structure brake
alone 2.10. Stacked they are 5.66 and 3.75, not the 7.95 and 5.00 you get by adding. The lab's
habitual quote (stacked minus switch) is 55% and 40% of each brake's true standalone price.
In 2008 the switch was out on 252 of 253 sessions and **not one** brake cash-day fell on a day it
was still invested. A crash brake is free in a crash and expensive in a calm market.
Scope: of the 21 methods with dev trials across `market-trend-switch`,
`volatility-term-structure-gate`, `macro-regime-gate` and `stock-switch-timing`, 8 carry an arm
invested >= 98% of the time and 13 do not. That cutoff is soft — 12 at >= 0.97, 3 at >= 0.99.
M0396 is queued to retrofit the arm by amendment: no method id, no test look.

**3. Five spent test looks have closed 39.5% of the dev-eligible lab.** Of 114 dev-eligible
methods, 45 are kin-blocked: 18 by family or ancestry, 3 by a shared ingredient, **24 by behaviour
alone** — so 27 are called clean by a names-and-lineage read. Kin is NOT family+ancestry; it is
family, ancestry, blend ingredients AND behaviour (residual monthly correlation >= 0.85 over >= 36
shared months). Use `hardgate.failed_kin(conn, id)`; do not reimplement it.
The chassis is the kinship, not the idea: any long-only twenty-name book rebalanced monthly behind
the market switch moves nearly identically to the others of that shape. **Check promotability with
`failed_kin` BEFORE reserving an idea** — this batch spent two of four slots on ideas that ran
clean to dev-eligible and were then refused. Insight 776 (supersedes 771, which had 21).

## Traps this session hit, so the next one does not

- **`trials.curve_json` is the FUNDED curve.** Annualizing it directly gives ~28%/yr for what is
  essentially SPY. The one de-funding path is `regime.defunded(curve, deposits)` with deposits from
  `hardgate.trial_deposits`; `dressing.leg_of(conn, row)` hands you both. `trials.cagr` is also
  deposit-contaminated; the money figure is `trial_funding.mwr`. The coordinator got this wrong
  once and produced a completely false decomposition before catching it.
- **Backticks in a `git commit -m` string are shell-substituted** and silently delete words. This
  killed M0385 and M0386 historically and mangled a commit here. Write the message to a file and
  use `git commit -F`.
- **Never pipe a lab command through `grep`** — the pipe owns the exit code, and a retry on a
  `lab note` double-appends permanently. Redirect to a file and check `$?`.
- **Two lab tests pinned a firing population that every batch re-rolls** and were red at preflight:
  the buy-signal census test (pinned "1 distinct book from 4 methods") and the money-column
  tolerance (0.00025, calibrated at 761 trials, exceeded at 1212). Both repaired to post-run truth
  in commit `cd0e831` at the start of this session. Expect this class again.
- **`lab stage` targets the main checkout even from a worktree**, and a lab command migrates
  `lab.sqlite` on connect, so even a read dirties it. Open it `file:lab/lab.sqlite?mode=ro` for
  reads.
