The lab's previous best shape-matched placebo was M0241's cohort mirror (m0241.placebo_book): for
each trailing calendar month it counted how many eligible members really split that month and drew
that many names in a hash order seeded on that month. Its own docstring claimed the monthly draws
were stable enough that turnover matched. Measured, it lands at 1.372x trades and 1.131x turnover
-- inside the refuse band but OUTSIDE the 0.80-1.25 match band. So the state of the art was a
warn, and a rule that merely imitated it would not have cleared the bar.

control_draw.py derives the churn instead of imitating it: at each decision session it recomputes
the real arm's own selection at this and at the previous decision session, takes the entry count e
as the size of the set difference, keeps n - e of its own held names in a date-free hash order,
and draws e fresh names from the same eligible pool -- with every non-held name sorted ahead of
every just-dropped holding, so a name the book has just dropped is never taken straight back. The
entry count per rebalance is matched BY CONSTRUCTION, not empirically, which is why it holds at
both poles of the failure rather than at one.

Measured on the two poles, where the hand-chosen draws read 0.07x/0.12x and 3.01x/2.98x:
  M0227-S20-PLC      0.99x trades  0.99x turnover
  M0243-NEAR20-PLC   1.03x trades  1.03x turnover
Across all thirteen re-drawn arms, ten land inside 0.80-1.25 on both axes, with trade ratios from
0.95 to 1.03 and turnover ratios from 0.84 to 1.03 -- against M0241's 1.372x.

The three that miss say where the rule reaches and where it does not, and all three misses are
measurements rather than failures:
  - Equal-slot chassis: it works on both axes, because turnover follows the trade ratio
    arithmetically. Seven of the thirteen arms are of this kind and all seven land ok.
  - Weight-varying chassis: it closes the TRADE axis and not the TURNOVER axis. A blind draw from
    a sized pool is flatter than the real top-N, so its weights cross the book engine's
    1%-of-equity resize band at a different rate on the same names. M0221-E50 pays 1.60x turnover
    on identical names and identical trades; M0221-C50-PLC pays 1.95x on 1.03x name changes, and
    M0201's two seeds land at 0.794 and 0.839 -- the same residual in the LOW direction, with two
    seeds of one rule straddling the bar.
  - Books whose churn cannot be reconstructed from one unrolled step of their keep band:
    matched_names has no record of what the real book held, so it re-derives the previous held set
    from the plain top-N. A freshly-taken top-20 is ranked 1-20 and a band that drops a name only
    past rank 40 drops almost none of it, while the real held set has drifted toward the edge and
    falls out far faster. Replayed over all 169 of M0208's decisions: 304 estimated entries
    against the real book's 624, 0.487x, uniform across 1996-2002, 2003-2008 and 2009-2015 (0.444
    / 0.509 / 0.500). The replay reproduces the real 624 exactly, so the diagnostic is faithful.

The transferable rule: a control's re-key period must be derived from the book it controls, each
rebalance, from that book's own calendar. Choosing the period by hand produced BOTH families of
failure in this lab -- sticky at 0.07x and churny at 5.20x -- from the same author correcting the
first by hand and overshooting into the second.

And what a matched draw is worth, beyond clearing a gate: it makes the opponent DEFINED. The
thirteen handicapped controls earned 4.381% to 11.381% a year (sd 1.918); their thirteen honest
replacements earn 6.047% to 9.532% (sd 0.988), 3.77x less variance. On the two chassis carrying
two seeds of one control, the handicapped seeds sit 2.190 and 1.737 points apart and the honest
ones 0.079 and 0.187. What an honest control buys is not a better margin in a known direction --
it is a margin whose opponent is not arbitrary.
